# Android 16 ROM + PhhIms 0.2.3

> **Warning: this was slop coded. Back up your data before flashing and use it at your own risk.**

The exact ROM and module used for the SM-G781B/r8q Aussie Broadband/Optus test. SMS and ordinary calls with two-way audio were reported; LTE IMS registration, cellular data, airplane recovery and module disable/re-enable were checked.

Install the ROM first, patch the unmodified boot on your own phone with official Magisk 31.1, then install the companion IMS module. IMS is not embedded in the ROM ZIP. Follow [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/testingraphael/crdroid-r8q-ims/blob/android16-ims/INSTALL.md) and verify SHA256SUMS.

Emergency calling has not been validated. Do not call 000 or another emergency number as a test.

Credits and source revisions are in [SOURCES.md](https://github.com/testingraphael/crdroid-r8q-ims/blob/android16-ims/SOURCES.md).
